ONE PRODUCTION, FOUR PLACES

Your idea moves between Studio, ComfyUI, your assistant and your phone.

Start a production in Studio, from your phone, or ask Claude or Codex to start it. Every shot is a real ComfyUI graph you can open and change, and every result comes back to the same film.

Produce a video →
  1. ✦StudioPick a format, type the idea. Produce plans the beats and runs them one by one.
  2. ⌘ComfyUIEach keyframe and clip is a pinned, editable graph on your GPU, or on Comfy Cloud.
  3. ◎Your assistantClaude or Codex can create, check and continue the same production through MCP.
  4. ▣Your phoneScan a code on your Wi-Fi to start, approve or remake shots from the couch.
  5. ▶A finished cutNarration, captions and a rough-cut MP4 you can fine-tune in Timeline.
