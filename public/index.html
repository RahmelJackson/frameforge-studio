A SMALL STUDIO, ON YOUR DESK

Make the next frame.

An independent, local alternative inspired by cinematic AI workspaces.

01 / CREATE

Give the scene a direction.

Choose a reference, one camera movement, and a short description of the action. Begin with a 2-second preview. The selected format center-crops your image.

02 / REVIEW

Check the actual video.

The verifier decodes every frame and checks size, frame rate, and length. It flags near-black frames and very low motion. Watch for identity drift, flicker, geometry changes, and whether the requested movement happened.

03 / REFINE

Change one thing.

Reuse a shot's settings and seed, make one deliberate change, then compare. Keep an approved take as your reference. Technical checks do not judge storytelling or taste.

ENGINE / WAN 2.2

Know the limits.

This version creates silent, short clips using open Wan weights. Quick mode uses prompt guidance. Camera mode uses native conditioning and still needs visual review. Proprietary Higgsfield weights, native audio, and character training are outside this version.
