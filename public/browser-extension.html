Frameforge Quick

Right-click any image on the web.

Remix it, select part of it with SAM 3, make it a video (or a vertical one), animate it with LTX 2.5, use it as a character, or turn it into 3D. The image goes straight from your browser to your own studio, and the right tool opens with it attached.

Download the extension (.zip)
  1. Unzip it anywhere you like, for example Documents.
  2. Open your extensions page: type chrome://extensions (Chrome, Brave) or edge://extensions (Edge) in the address bar.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right in Chrome, left side in Edge).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped frameforge-quick folder.
  5. Right-click any image and choose Frameforge.

Away from home? Click the extension's icon and set your PC's Tailscale address. Use images you have the right to use, and respect the people in them.
